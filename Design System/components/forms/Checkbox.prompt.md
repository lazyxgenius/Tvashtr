Checkbox or radio with an optional label and description. Pass `type="radio"` for a round single-choice control.

```jsx
<Checkbox label="Remember this device" defaultChecked />
<Checkbox label="Email me digests" description="A calm weekly summary, nothing more." />
<Checkbox type="radio" name="plan" label="Studio" defaultChecked />
<Checkbox type="radio" name="plan" label="Team" />
```
