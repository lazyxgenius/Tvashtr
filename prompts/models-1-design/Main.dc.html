Every model starts with its tests

Pick one job you want done well, like sorting claims into teams. Write down real cases with the right answers. Tvashtr runs them on the models you have keys for and shows which does best, and what it costs.

  1. Step 1
    Describe the job
    One sentence, and the kind of answer you expect.
  2. Step 2
    Add tests
    Type them in or import a CSV. Keep adding as you learn.
  3. Step 3
    Run them
    On the models you hold keys for. Compare any two side by side.
